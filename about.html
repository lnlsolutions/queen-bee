Our story

From one wall to the whole house

Most painting companies stop at the paint. We kept going, because homeowners kept asking us to fix what was underneath it.

The first fifteen years were walls. Interior repaints, exterior stucco, cabinet doors sprayed in the driveway. Doing it well and on schedule is how a painting company survives in a market with three hundred of them.

Then the calls changed. Can you patch this ceiling? Can you open up this kitchen? So we added drywall and texture, then cabinet refinishing, then tile, then full kitchen and bath remodels with plumbing and electrical pulled by licensed partners we have worked with for years.

That is what we are now: a small, owner-run company that can take a room from cracked drywall to finished, and a house from tired to sold. One crew lead on every job, one point of contact, one price agreed before the first drop cloth goes down.

We are bilingual on purpose. Half of the Valley builds in Spanish, and a homeowner should never have to wonder whether the crew understood what was agreed. Our estimates, our walkthroughs and our texts come in the language you prefer.
